Welcome to Night Security Entropy Creator

Rules:

  • Two players take turns selecting one of four 8x4 matrices.
  • Each player selects twice (two rounds).
  • You have 5 seconds per turn to choose a matrix.
  • If you don't select in time, a low-entropy matrix is chosen, likely causing a loss.
  • The goal is to maximize the entropy of your selections.
  • The player with higher total entropy wins!
